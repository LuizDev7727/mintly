# What's new

You can now connect an Instagram account to Mintly and publish content to it just like any other channel — no more switching apps to post manually.

- Added Instagram as a supported network, with its own icon across the app.
- Posts scheduled for Instagram go through the same review and publishing flow as your other channels.

## Fixes

- Fixed a bug where the Instagram network icon and type were missing in a few places after the initial rollout.
